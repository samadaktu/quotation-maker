Quran Recitation with Tajweed

★ ★ ★ ★ ★ 5.0
by Khalid Al-Faisal

Master the art of Quranic recitation with proper Tajweed rules. This comprehensive course covers pronunciation, articulation points, and the beautiful rhythms of Quranic recitation. Perfect for children and beginners who want to recite the Quran correctly and beautifully.

Through interactive lessons and practice sessions, students will learn the fundamental rules of Tajweed, including Makharij (articulation points), Sifaat (characteristics of letters), and various types of Madd (elongation). Each lesson is designed to build confidence and fluency in Quranic recitation.
